# Reflection: Building My Career Identity

One thing I've already noticed about myself is that my tendency toward perfectionism could become one of the biggest challenges on this journey. It's easy to get caught up in trying to do everything perfectly, but I'm learning that progress is more important than perfection. For now, I'm choosing not to stress about having everything figured out.

Today, I spent some time watching videos and researching the concept of building a Career Identity. I wanted to understand how professionals intentionally shape their careers rather than simply moving from one role or certification to the next.

After learning more about it, I created my own Career Identity roadmap focused on Cloud Security. The process helped me gain more clarity about where I want to go, the skills I need to develop, and the kind of professional I aspire to become.

What makes this even more valuable is that it isn't just for me. I designed it in a way that can also help someone who is interested in starting a Cloud Security career but isn't sure where to begin.

If you're curious, check it out:

- [Monobong's Career Identity for Cloud Security (2026)](./Career_Identity_Cloud_Security_2026.pdf)

### My Key Takeaway

Today's lesson for me wasn't about a cloud service or security tool. It was about direction. Before focusing on certifications, projects, or technologies, it's important to understand the destination you're working toward.
